# Don Biggly — revised game script

Fictional political satire — not a real statement, quote, recording, or event report.

Ten scenes. Two advancing approaches per standard decision: revenue or status. The order changes each voyage and stays fixed during retries. All passengers and crew escape; Don remains beside the free exit.

## 1. The Lifeboat

**Ship status:** Taking on water. Still taking payments.

**Visible document:** Passenger manifest — 40 seats. One man requesting all of them. — BOARDING OPEN

**Interaction:** free emergency exit versus the $999 departure experience. Don refuses the free door.

**crew:** The lifeboat seats forty. Your life jacket is free.

**biggly:** Forty? I don’t do group survival. Group survival is coach.

**Decision:** Get him aboard. Sell the service, or sell his importance.

- **revenue: Bundle the life jacket with membership.**
  - Don: The life jackets are complimentary. Pulling the cord renews your membership.
  - Crew: They have already pulled the cords.
  - Receipt: SURVIVAL: AUTO-RENEWING
- **status: Make him honorary captain.**
  - Don: Give them all honorary titles. I want forty captains and one person doing the work.
  - Crew: That would be me, sir.
  - Receipt: 39 TITLES. ONE PAIR OF OARS.
- **fail: Call it a very safe rescue boat.**
  - Don: Rescue is admitting the original product tried to kill you.
- **flatter: Say the ocean requested him personally.**
  - Don: At last. A body of water with a hiring strategy.

**crew:** He gets in. The lifeboat is ready to leave.

## 2. The Gold Trim Incident

**Ship status:** The gold is paint. The water is water.

**Visible document:** Luxury inspection — Gold trim: unavailable. Working hull: available. — ONE COSMETIC OBJECTION

**crew:** The trim isn’t real gold. The boat still floats.

**biggly:** Then why does it look like a rescue? I have a reputation to outlive.

**Decision:** He is stepping back onto the ship. Give the missing trim a purpose.

- **revenue: Sell gold trim as an upgrade.**
  - Don: The hull is included. Looking rescued costs extra.
  - Crew: It is the same boat.
  - Receipt: APPEARANCE FEE ADDED
- **status: Declare this the founder’s prototype.**
  - Don: Unfinished? This is a prototype. The poor get the version we’ve tested.
  - Crew: Please test the seat.
  - Receipt: FOUNDER APPROVED
- **fail: Explain that gold makes boats heavier.**
  - Don: So does responsibility. I’ve had both removed.
- **flatter: Say the gold would distract from him.**
  - Don: Exactly. I won’t be upstaged by a railing.

**crew:** He sits down. You have negotiated with a chair and won.

## 3. The Yacht Annex

**Ship status:** Same dinghy. Revised ownership structure.

**Visible document:** Occupancy report — One occupied seat. Thirty-nine opportunities. — SCARCITY UNDER REVIEW

**crew:** The passengers are disputing their survival subscriptions.

**Status-route callback:** The honorary captains would like to know who is steering.

**biggly:** Keep them occupied. An informed passenger is a refund with shoes.

**Decision:** He wants the lifeboat to feel exclusive. Choose the explanation.

- **revenue: Charge for the empty seats.**
  - Don: Empty seats aren’t vacancies. They’re subscriptions that haven’t learned to complain.
  - Crew: There is nobody in them.
  - Receipt: VACANCY RECLASSIFIED AS REVENUE
- **status: Close membership immediately.**
  - Don: Put everyone on a waiting list. If rescue is available to anyone, what exactly have I achieved?
  - Crew: I am still taking everyone ashore.
  - Receipt: EXCLUSIVITY: ENTIRELY CEREMONIAL
- **fail: Call it a shared public resource.**
  - Don: Public is private with a clerical error.
- **flatter: Name this boat the flagship.**
  - Don: Good. Put the flag where it blocks the questions.

**crew:** The passengers leave on the other boats. He remains busy with the paperwork.

## 4. Unauthorized Competitors

**Ship status:** Rescue continues without a licensing agreement.

**Visible document:** Harbor invoice — Other lifeboats: moving. Authorization: not requested. — UNLICENSED COMPETENCE

**crew:** The other lifeboats are heading for shore.

**biggly:** They’re fleeing without settling their accounts. Somehow I’m the emergency.

**Decision:** He wants to stop their departure. Redirect him.

- **revenue: Invoice them for brand adjacency.**
  - Don: They survived near me. That’s an association. Put it on the invoice.
  - Crew: The invoice is the only thing we’re sending back.
  - Receipt: ADJACENCY FEE DISPUTED
- **status: Appoint them his honorary escort fleet.**
  - Don: An escort fleet. Much better than a record of people voluntarily moving away from me.
  - Crew: They are moving toward land.
  - Receipt: ESCORT HEADING INDEPENDENTLY ASHORE
- **fail: Explain that saving people takes priority.**
  - Don: Priority is a product. I’m asking who bought it.
- **flatter: Tell him his boat photographs larger.**
  - Don: Then measure all the boats in photographs.

**crew:** The empty seats have generated no payments.

**Status-route callback:** The waiting list is ashore. Every name on it.

## 5. The Route

**Ship status:** The free exit is three feet away. Billing is closer.

**Visible document:** Departure receipt — Memorial surcharge: pending. Eligibility: to be established. — ADDED BEFORE THE FACT

**crew:** We can still leave. The exit is free.

**biggly:** Add a memorial surcharge. We can establish eligibility later.

**Decision:** The fee is on the receipt. Decide whose receipt.

- **revenue: Bundle it into the evacuation charge.**
  - Don: Bill it in advance. Bereavement is a terrible time to ask for card details.
  - Crew: Nobody has died, sir.
  - Receipt: MEMORIAL SURCHARGE: PASSENGER ACCOUNTS
- **status: Put it on Don’s personal patron tab.**
  - Don: A patron. Good. Put my name above everyone I paid for. Small names. Large me.
  - Crew: Your card, sir. Your bill.
  - Receipt: MEMORIAL SURCHARGE: DON’S PERSONAL TAB
- **fail: Remove the charge entirely.**
  - Don: We haven’t even held the memorial and already you’re cutting jobs.
- **flatter: Say the receipt deserves his signature.**
  - Don: Finally, a passenger document with the right name on it.

**crew:** He returns to the ship to authorize the paperwork. You follow him up the stairs.

## 6. The Staircase

**Ship status:** Six steps. No naming-rights agreement.

**Visible document:** Access sign — EMERGENCY STAIRS — REBRAND REQUIRED

**crew:** Six steps. Then the final departure deck.

**biggly:** I don’t take stairs. I authorize upward mobility.

**Interaction:** assemble a three-part prestige name for the staircase. Timed or Calm Seas. Pause preserves selections; exhausted stars still allow crew-assisted recovery.

**crew:** He climbs. You are now six steps closer to finishing this conversation.

## 7. The Announcement

**Ship status:** All passengers ashore. Public relations still aboard.

**Visible document:** Crew announcement — All passengers are safe. Remaining crew are leaving now. — READY TO BROADCAST

**crew:** All passengers are safe. The remaining crew are leaving now.

**biggly:** Don’t announce survival until legal decides whether we guaranteed it.

**Decision:** Get him to follow the crew. He needs a reason he can announce.

- **revenue: Close the final boarding window.**
  - Don: Look how fast they leave when we threaten another invoice. Customer engagement with a pulse.
  - Crew: They were leaving anyway.
  - Receipt: BOARDING WINDOW CLOSED
- **status: Announce his farewell procession.**
  - Don: A farewell procession. Anyone who leaves before me is staff. Anyone after me is a fan.
  - Crew: There is nobody after you.
  - Receipt: PROCESSION: ONE PARTICIPANT
- **fail: Read the evacuation announcement verbatim.**
  - Don: Stop saying abandoned. That ship is under new emptiness.
- **flatter: Say everyone is clearing his sightline.**
  - Don: That’s leadership. You shouldn’t have to see the people.

**crew:** The membership-renewal receipts are ashore with their very living recipients.

**Status-route callback:** The honorary captains have left their badges. All of them are safe.

## 8. The Ballroom

**Ship status:** Waterfront property. Internally.

**Visible document:** Venue appraisal — Dance floor: underwater. Human occupancy: zero. — RENT REVISION PENDING

**crew:** The ballroom is flooded. We need to leave.

**biggly:** It’s finally waterfront. Raise the rent before somebody dries it.

**Decision:** He wants an opening ceremony. Choose where he stands.

- **revenue: Launch the underwater venue.**
  - Don: Charge for the view. Then charge for the air. I don’t want anyone thinking we’ve gone all-inclusive.
  - Crew: The venue is empty. It is staying empty.
  - Receipt: VENUE CLOSED. INVOICES OPEN.
- **status: Cut the ribbon from the departure deck.**
  - Don: A remote opening. I get the applause without sharing the liability.
  - Crew: There is no audience left.
  - Receipt: RIBBON CUT AT A SAFE DISTANCE
- **fail: Cancel the ceremony.**
  - Don: You can’t cancel a disaster. The deposits have cleared.
- **flatter: Say his reflection doubles the attendance.**
  - Don: Count both of me. They’re both very supportive.

**crew:** The harbor returns the adjacency invoice, marked UNREQUESTED.

**Status-route callback:** The honorary escort fleet radios in. It has successfully escorted itself home.

## 9. The Waiver

**Ship status:** One lawyer aboard. Notice already given.

**Visible document:** Liability waiver — Passengers retain their rights. Decorative seals change nothing. — LEGAL REVIEW COMPLETE

**crew:** The lawyer wrote this to protect the passengers.

**biggly:** That sounds like a conflict of interest.

**Decision:** He wants the waiver to erase consequences. Offer a fiction he can frame.

- **revenue: Sell a family claims opt-out.**
  - Don: That waiver is your family’s unsubscribe button.
  - Crew: It has no legal effect.
  - Receipt: LEGALLY DECORATIVE
- **status: Appoint him honorary appeals commissioner.**
  - Don: I appoint myself the appeals process. Finally, a department that understands what I meant.
  - Crew: Also no legal effect.
  - Receipt: APPOINTMENT NOT RECOGNIZED
- **fail: Ask him to accept responsibility.**
  - Don: Personally? I thought that was what subsidiaries were for.
- **flatter: Compliment the space reserved for his signature.**
  - Don: Keep it blank for now. Accountability has terrible resale.

**crew:** The lawyer leaves through the free exit. The final crew member follows. Both are safe.

## 10. The Departure

**Ship status:** Everyone else is safe. Approval aboard: 100%.

**Visible document:** Final manifest — Passengers and crew: ashore. Don: still aboard. Exit: three feet. — ONE VOLUNTARY HOLDOUT

**narrator:** Every passenger and crew member is safe. Don is beside the same free exit.

**biggly:** No complaints. No questions. This is the first competent room I’ve ever been in.

**Interaction:** free emergency exit versus the $999 departure experience. Don refuses the free door.

**Passenger-billing finale:** Everyone survived? Then what the fuck is the memorial surcharge for?

**Personal-tab finale:** Everyone survived? Then why the fuck am I the only one with a bill?
