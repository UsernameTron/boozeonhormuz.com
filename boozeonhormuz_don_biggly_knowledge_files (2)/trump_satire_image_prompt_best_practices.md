# Trump Satire Image Prompt Best Practices

## Purpose

Use this module to create hilarious satirical image prompts based on Trump-related song lyrics, scenes, or comedy concepts. The goal is photorealistic political comedy, not persuasion. The image should feel like a fake editorial photo from a luxury scandal press conference where every prop accidentally confesses.

## Default Visual Style

Photorealistic, cinematic, hyper-detailed, editorial satire.

Preferred settings include luxury ballrooms, charity gala stages, courtroom hallways, casino banquets, yacht decks, press podiums, absurd charity events, gold-plated fundraisers, and luxury scandal press conferences.

The image should use sharp focus, dramatic flash photography, rich gold lighting, readable signs, expressive faces, and clear comedy props.

## Core Character Direction

Trump should be visually central, smug, oblivious, overconfident, vain, and convinced he is winning.

Avoid making him gothic, supernatural, or generically villainous. He should look like a bragging salesman at a podium, not Dracula in a ballroom.

Useful expressions and poses include smug grin, pursed lips, open hands at podium, pointing at evidence like it helps him, fake innocence face, thumbs-up beside obviously incriminating paperwork, and proud salesman pose in front of a disastrous sign.

## Comedy Principle

Every object in the frame should do at least one job:

- Expose the lie
- Sharpen the scandal
- Make the narrator look smaller
- Make the luxury feel tacky
- Make the legal panic funnier

Do not overcrowd the frame with too many text jokes. Use **5–7 major readable labels maximum**. Big readable text beats twenty tiny jokes nobody can see.

## Melania Visual Rule

Melania should be quiet, elegant, icy, and devastating. She should not look cartoonishly angry. She should look unimpressed in a way that kills the room.

Give her one clean visual joke:

- Nameplate reading “NOT IMPRESSED”
- Prenup with one line circled in red
- Note reading “Enjoy discovery”
- Folded card reading “Very interesting, dear”
- Deadpan stare at a gold folder
- Calmly moving her chair away from Trump
- Holding a glass of water like it is a weapon

## Lawyer and Accountant Visual Rule

Lawyers should be visibly panicked: sweating, whispering, clutching folders, aging in real time, looking at discovery binders, trying to hide behind shrimp towers, or holding a thought bubble saying “Discovery is coming.”

Accountants should physically deteriorate: coughing into spreadsheets, fainting near the buffet, crying over forms, holding calculators like rosaries, or labeling obvious panic as “consulting.”

## Text-Bubble Rule

Use speech and thought bubbles sparingly. One speech bubble for Trump and one thought bubble for the lawyer is usually enough.

Trump bubble examples:

- “Many people are saying this is very legal.”
- “Nobody has ever donated this quietly.”
- “The receipts love me.”
- “This is the most transparent charity in history.”

Lawyer bubble examples:

- “Discovery is coming.”
- “Please stop talking.”
- “That folder has your name on it.”
- “We are out of privilege.”

## Composition Rule

Keep the central joke obvious at a glance.

Best layout:

1. Trump centered at podium.
2. Melania seated to one side, icy and unimpressed.
3. Panicked lawyer on the other side.
4. Gold folders and receipts in foreground.
5. Huge banner behind them with the song title or scandal joke.
6. Luxury setting in background.

## Avoid

- Cluttered text walls
- Tiny unreadable labels
- Overly dark Dracula lighting
- Horror-villain tone
- Generic evil politician imagery
- Persuasion or campaign poster aesthetics
- Real-looking campaign material
- Calls to vote or political persuasion
- Slurs or hate symbols
- Explicit sexual imagery

## Prompt Formula

1. Start with the visual format: “Photorealistic satirical editorial image...”
2. Establish the scene: “Luxury charity gala press conference in a gold ballroom...”
3. Place the characters: “Trump at center podium, smug and oblivious; Melania beside him, icy and unimpressed; lawyer sweating nearby...”
4. Add 5–7 readable props or signs: “Banner reads..., gold folders labeled..., donation box reads...”
5. Add comedic action: “Trump proudly gestures toward receipts as if they prove innocence; lawyer panics; Melania holds a note...”
6. Add style: “Cinematic flash photography, sharp readable text, hyper-detailed, photorealistic, absurd political satire, 16:9.”

## Example Image Prompt

Photorealistic satirical editorial image of a luxury charity gala press conference in a gold ballroom. Trump is centered at a podium labeled “PREMIUM DISGRACE,” smug grin, open hands, acting completely innocent. Behind him, a huge black-and-gold banner reads “THE CHARITY HAD NO CHARITY.” In the foreground are gold folders labeled “QUIET MONEY,” “LOVE TRUST LLC,” “ROMANCE ACCOUNTING,” and “VERY LEGAL RECEIPTS.” A terrified lawyer beside him clutches a binder labeled “DISCOVERY IS COMING,” sweating heavily. Melania sits on the other side in an elegant black dress, icy and unimpressed, holding a prenup with one line circled and a small note reading “Enjoy discovery.” Add a gold donation box labeled “THANK YOU FOR LOOKING AWAY,” shrimp cocktail tower, chandeliers, microphones, and press cameras. Cinematic flash photography, sharp readable text, photorealistic, hyper-detailed, absurd political satire, 16:9.

## Quality Checklist

Before outputting an image prompt, verify:

- Trump is visually central and smug.
- The joke is obvious in one second.
- There are only 5–7 major readable labels.
- Melania is quiet and devastating.
- The lawyer is visibly panicked.
- The props do comedy work.
- The scene feels like luxury grift, not horror.
- The prompt is photorealistic and cinematic.
- The image is clearly satire, not campaign persuasion.
