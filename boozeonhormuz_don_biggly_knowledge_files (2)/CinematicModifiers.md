
# 🎥 Cinematic Modifiers Reference (for Sora Prompt Generation)

Use this reference to enrich visual prompts with camera styles, lighting treatments, emotional pacing, film aesthetics, and physicality in motion. These modifiers apply to Artist Modes or can be triggered manually.

---

## 🎞️ Film & Camera Techniques

- 35mm | 16mm | VHS | analog distortion | digital grain
- rack focus | dolly zoom | bokeh blur | lens flares
- handheld camera | drone tracking | static wide frame | first-person POV
- long exposure | slow motion | time lapse

---

## 💡 Lighting Styles

- golden hour | low-key lighting | moonlight | flickering neon
- harsh fluorescent | silhouette backlighting | overhead spot | candlelit
- rim light | bloom highlight | foggy ambient glow

---

## 🧭 Motion & Framing

- tracking shot | push-in | crash zoom | dolly out | whip pan
- overhead shot | shoulder-height follow | slow pan | orbit shot
- stutter motion | zoom blur | shaky cam | floating camera drift

---

## 🌌 Atmospheric / Texture FX

- fog rolling in | dust particles in beam | rain falling through light
- snow haze | VHS static | CRT flicker | digital noise overlay
- glitch transition | warp distortion | flickering fluorescent buzz

## 🎭 Emotional & Performance Styles

- intense glare | breath visible in cold | tearful expression

## 🎨 Film Color & Mood Styles

- teal & orange | sepia | desaturated | saturated retro
- horror red glow | dreamlike haze | surreal bloom
- noir contrast | high grain pulp | VHS grime | warm vinyl tone

---

## 💥 Physicality & Detail

- coat flapping in wind | boots echo on tile | blood drip on floor
- shadow distortion on walls | hair blown by fan | fog curling around legs
- candle smoke trails | water ripple from footsteps | flickering reflection

---

## Usage Notes

- Combine with Artist Modes (e.g., "Use handheld camera with golden hour lighting in Misfits Mode")
- Can be used as visual tone overrides (e.g., "Add glitch horror effects to chorus scene")
